03 — About

I'm here for the people who make this town work.

New Bern is full of small businesses run by owners who are too busy running them to talk about them. So I do the talking. I'm out at the shops, the counters, and the corners of this town with my phone out, showing people what's worth their Saturday.

That's the whole thing, in three parts. I make content about New Bern and the Crystal Coast. I run Shoreline Socials, my marketing agency, where I handle social, content, and branding for local businesses. And I built Beyond the Bear Town so this town has a place to celebrate itself.

Different things on paper. Same job, really. I tell New Bern's stories to New Bern.
