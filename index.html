About Sophia

Local roots, real results

I'm a Crystal Coast girl through and through. For years I ran a bakery in Bridgeton with my mom, which is how half of New Bern knows me, and that same love for people and place is what I pour into marketing today.

I founded Shoreline Socials to give local businesses honest, done-for-you marketing without the jargon or the lock-in. I also built and run Beyond the Bear Town, a 1,400-member community that celebrates what makes New Bern tick, and I lead creative for the Ubuntu apparel brand.

Whether it's your social, your brand, or your whole online presence, I treat your business like it's my own. Because around here, your reputation is everything.
